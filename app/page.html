            How it works

            1. The first time for an account: drop every bank statement from 1 April (box 2), the Eazypay reports if the account has them (box 3), the Bank Book from 1 April made by the Bank Book Linearizer (box 4) and last year's signed BRS (box 5).
            2. Choose the month under "BRS as on". The BRS always runs from 1 April to the end of that month. Download the BRS Excel.
            3. Decide. In the BRS Excel open the To review sheet and fill the Decision column (Accept, Reject, Keep open) or type the other side in Match with. Save the file.
            4. Every month after that: drop last month's BRS Excel (box 1), the new month's statement (box 2), the new month's Eazypay reports (box 3) and the Bank Book from 1 April (box 4). The earlier statements, the earlier reports, last year's BRS and your decisions are carried inside the BRS Excel.
            5. Read the result. A green PASS means every line agrees with the running balance and with the totals the bank prints. An amber CHECK tells you what to look at. If a file is locked, you will be asked for its password; it is used once and not kept.
            6. Keep the original PDFs and reports. Dropping all of them from 1 April always works, with or without last month's BRS Excel.

            Scanned or photographed statements cannot be read, because they contain a picture and no text.
