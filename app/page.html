How it works

  1. Drop the PDFs. Any order. If a file is locked, you will be asked for its password; the password is used once and not kept.
  2. Read the result. A green PASS means every line agrees with the running balance and with the totals the bank prints. An amber CHECK tells you the page and line to look at.
  3. Download the Excel file. One file per bank account, with all the months joined in date order.
  4. To match with the books (optional). Drop the Bank Book workbooks made by the Bank Book Linearizer for the same account, and the Eazypay reports if the account has them. A second Excel file lists what matched and what needs a look.
  5. To decide (optional). In that second Excel file, open the To review sheet and fill the Decision column (Accept, Reject, Keep open) or type the other side in Match with. Save it. Next time drop it here with the other files: your decisions are applied again and only the undecided items come back.

Scanned or photographed statements cannot be read, because they contain a picture and no text.
